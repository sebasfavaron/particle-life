How Particle Life works

1. A living canvas

1↔2↔3

Colored types follow their rules at the same time.

2. Live controls

Radius, damping, force, dt, speed, wrap and zoom update the running world.

Example: Force .02 → .08 makes attraction and repulsion faster.

Hold Interaction radius to see its reach. + makes the virtual canvas larger.

3. New world controls

Particles, Color classes and Reset particles create a new population.

Seed controls the next new population. Same seed + settings = same start.
